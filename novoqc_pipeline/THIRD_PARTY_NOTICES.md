# Third-party code

`novoqc/depthcharge_new` and the sequencing model retain the modified
Depthcharge/Transformer implementation distributed with pi-NovoQC. Depthcharge
is distributed under Apache-2.0; its license is retained as
`novoqc/depthcharge_new/LICENSE-depthcharge`
(https://github.com/wfondrie/depthcharge). The relative
Transformer components derive from the work of Robert Csordas and retain its
MIT license in `novoqc/depthcharge_new/LICENSE`.

Pyteomics and PyProteinInference are installed as dependencies rather than
vendored. They and all other dependencies retain their respective licenses.
